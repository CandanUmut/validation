title: How layer 2 hurts throughput
source: v1
points:
  - RLC retransmissions add delay. TCP sees delay and jitter and slows down.
  - Out-of-order delivery makes TCP send duplicate ACKs and cut its window.
  - A stalled RLC window (waiting for one missing PDU) blocks everything behind it.
  - UDP iperf shows what the radio can do. TCP iperf shows what the user gets. Run both.
  - If UDP is fine and TCP is poor, look at latency, jitter, reordering, MTU and window sizes before blaming the radio.
say: I separate radio capacity from transport behavior by testing UDP and TCP side by side.
