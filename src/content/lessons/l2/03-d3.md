title: How layer 2 hurts throughput
source: practice
points:
- <i>In practice:</i> RLC retransmissions add delay; TCP sees delay and jitter and slows down.
- <i>In practice:</i> out-of-order delivery makes TCP send duplicate ACKs and shrink its window.
- <i>In practice:</i> a stalled window waiting for one missing PDU blocks everything behind it.
- <i>In practice:</i> UDP iperf shows what the radio can carry; TCP iperf shows what a user gets. Run both.
- <i>In practice:</i> if UDP is fine and TCP is poor, look at latency, jitter, reordering, MTU and window sizes before blaming the radio.
say: I separate radio capacity from transport behaviour by testing UDP and TCP side by side.
trap: TCP behaviour is defined by the IETF, not 3GPP; a 3GPP log alone will not explain it. Capture IP traffic too.
