title: PDCP and SDAP
source: v1
points:
  - PDCP does <b>ciphering</b> for all bearers and <b>integrity protection</b> for signaling (and optionally for data in NR).
  - It does <b>header compression</b> (ROHC), which matters most for voice.
  - It reorders and removes duplicates, which matters at handover and with split bearers.
  - A <b>discard timer</b> drops packets that waited too long in the buffer.
  - NR adds <b>SDAP</b> above PDCP to map QoS flows to DRBs.
  - Security starts with the <b>Security Mode Command</b> at RRC. A cipher or integrity mismatch shows up as integrity check failures or garbage after decryption.
say: PDCP is security, compression and ordering. In NR, SDAP sits on top to map QoS flows to bearers.
trap: After handover, data stalls are often PDCP sequence number or key (KeNB/KgNB) problems, not radio problems.
