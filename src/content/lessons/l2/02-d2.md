title: PDCP and SDAP
source: research
points:
- PDCP does <b>ciphering</b>, <b>integrity protection</b> (always for signalling; optional for data bearers in NR, when connected to 5GC), and header compression (<b>ROHC</b>, plus Ethernet header compression).
- It numbers packets, <b>reorders</b> and delivers in order (or out of order if configured), removes duplicates, and routes or duplicates packets on split bearers.
- A <b>discard timer</b> drops packets that waited too long.
- NR adds <b>SDAP</b> above PDCP to map QoS flows to data radio bearers, in both directions.
- AS security starts with RRC <code>SecurityModeCommand</code> on SRB1. A key or algorithm mismatch shows up as integrity failures or garbage after deciphering.
say: PDCP is security, compression and ordering. SDAP on top maps QoS flows to bearers.
trap: <i>In practice:</i> data stalls right after handover are often PDCP sequence number or key problems, not radio problems.
