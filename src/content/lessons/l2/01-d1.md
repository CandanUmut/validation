title: RLC modes and ARQ
source: research
points:
- <b>TM</b> (transparent) for SRB0, paging and broadcast system information. <b>AM</b> for the other SRBs. Data bearers use <b>UM</b> or <b>AM</b>.
- 'UM: segmentation and reassembly, no retransmission. AM: adds ARQ, re-segmentation and duplicate detection. <i>In practice:</i> UM is typical for voice.'
- AM uses <b>status reports</b> (ACK/NACK of sequence numbers); the sender asks for one by <b>polling</b>, and the receiver can send one when it sees a gap.
- 'NR RLC timers: <code>t-PollRetransmit</code>, <code>t-StatusProhibit</code>, <code>t-Reassembly</code>.'
- <b>NR RLC does not reorder.</b> In NR, reordering and in-order delivery moved to PDCP (with its own <code>t-Reordering</code>). LTE RLC still reorders.
- When a PDU reaches <code>maxRetxThreshold</code> retransmissions, RLC tells RRC, and RRC declares <b>radio link failure</b>.
say: RLC AM is the reliable mode; at the maximum retransmissions the link is declared failed. In NR, ordering is PDCP's job.
trap: In NR, a 'reordering timer too short' problem is in PDCP's <code>t-Reordering</code>, not in RLC.
