title: RLC modes and ARQ
source: v1
points:
  - "<b>TM</b> (transparent): no header. Used for broadcast, paging, SRB0."
  - "<b>UM</b> (unacknowledged): segmentation, no retransmission. Used for voice and other delay-sensitive traffic."
  - "<b>AM</b> (acknowledged): segmentation plus retransmission. Used for SRBs and most data bearers."
  - AM works with <b>status reports</b> (ACK and NACK of sequence numbers). The sender asks for one with a <b>poll</b> bit.
  - "Key timers: <code>t-PollRetransmit</code>, <code>t-StatusProhibit</code>, and <code>t-Reordering</code> (LTE) or <code>t-Reassembly</code> (NR)."
  - If one PDU is retransmitted <code>maxRetxThreshold</code> times without success, RLC reports to RRC and that triggers <b>radio link failure</b>.
say: RLC AM is the reliable mode. If it hits the maximum retransmissions, the connection is declared failed.
trap: The reordering timer must be longer than the worst HARQ delay. If it is too short, RLC asks for retransmissions that were still on their way.
