title: The protocol stack, layer by layer
source: research
points:
- '<b>NAS</b> (UE to core, terminated in the AMF or MME): registration, authentication, security control, sessions. The base station only carries it.'
- '<b>RRC</b> (UE to gNB): system information, connection control, radio bearers, measurements, mobility.'
- '<b>SDAP</b> (NR user plane): maps QoS flows to data radio bearers.'
- '<b>PDCP</b>: ciphering, integrity, header compression, reordering and in-order delivery.'
- '<b>RLC</b>: segmentation and reassembly; in AM, ARQ retransmission.'
- '<b>MAC</b>: scheduling, HARQ, random access, multiplexing of logical channels.'
- '<b>PHY</b>: coding, modulation, OFDM, reference signals, measurements.'
- 'Control plane: NAS / RRC / PDCP / RLC / MAC / PHY. User plane: SDAP / PDCP / RLC / MAC / PHY.'
say: NAS talks to the core, RRC controls the radio link, PDCP secures, RLC makes it reliable, MAC schedules and does HARQ, PHY puts bits on the air.
trap: 'There are two retransmission layers: fast HARQ in MAC, slower ARQ in RLC that catches what HARQ missed.'
