title: Identifiers you will see in logs

points:
- '<b>Subscriber:</b> IMSI (LTE); SUPI and its concealed form <b>SUCI</b> (5G). Temporary: GUTI, 5G-GUTI, S-TMSI, 5G-S-TMSI.'
- <b>Radio (RNTI):</b> C-RNTI (the connection), RA-RNTI (random access response), TC-RNTI (temporary, during random access), P-RNTI (paging), SI-RNTI (system information).
- <b>Cell:</b> PCI (504 in LTE, 1008 in NR), global cell ID (ECGI, NCGI), TAC, PLMN (MCC + MNC).
- <b>Frequency:</b> EARFCN, NR-ARFCN, GSCN (NR synchronization raster), band (for example n77).
- <b>Bearer:</b> EPS bearer ID, DRB ID, LCID, QFI.
say: When I open a log I pin down PLMN, band, channel, PCI and RNTI first, so I know which cell and which connection I am reading.
trap: 'PCI is not unique: two nearby cells sharing a PCI (confusion or collision) break measurements and handover.'
