title: Identifiers you will see in logs
source: v1
points:
  - "<b>Subscriber:</b> IMSI (LTE), SUPI and its concealed form SUCI (5G). Temporary: GUTI, 5G-GUTI, S-TMSI."
  - <b>Radio (RNTI):</b> C-RNTI (your connection), RA-RNTI (random access response), TC-RNTI (temporary, during random access), P-RNTI (paging), SI-RNTI (system information).
  - "<b>Cell:</b> PCI (physical cell ID: 504 values in LTE, 1008 in NR), global cell ID (ECGI, NCGI), TAC (tracking area code), PLMN (MCC + MNC)."
  - <b>Frequency:</b> EARFCN (LTE channel number), NR-ARFCN, GSCN (NR sync raster), band number (for example band 25, n25, n77).
  - <b>Bearer:</b> EPS bearer ID, DRB ID, LCID (logical channel ID), QFI (QoS flow ID).
say: When I open a log, I first pin down PLMN, band, channel number, PCI and the RNTI, so I know exactly which cell and which connection I am reading.
trap: PCI is not unique. Two cells can share a PCI (PCI confusion or collision), which breaks handover.
