title: 'Channels: logical, transport, physical'
source: research
points:
- '<b>Logical channels</b> (what kind of information): BCCH broadcast, PCCH paging, CCCH common control, DCCH dedicated control, DTCH dedicated traffic.'
- '<b>Transport channels</b> (how MAC hands it to PHY): BCH, PCH, DL-SCH, UL-SCH, RACH.'
- <b>NR physical channels:</b> downlink PDSCH, PBCH, PDCCH; uplink PUSCH, PUCCH, PRACH.
- <b>DCI</b> (downlink control information) carries grants and assignments; <b>UCI</b> carries HARQ-ACK, scheduling requests and CSI.
- LTE also has PCFICH and PHICH; NR defines neither.
say: PDCCH tells the UE where its data is; PDSCH and PUSCH carry it; PUCCH carries ACKs, scheduling requests and channel reports.
trap: SIBs travel on PDSCH, not PBCH. Only the MIB is on PBCH.
