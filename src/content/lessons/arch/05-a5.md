title: "Channels: logical, transport, physical"
source: v1
points:
  - "<b>Logical channels</b> say what kind of information: BCCH (broadcast), PCCH (paging), CCCH (common control), DCCH (dedicated control), DTCH (dedicated traffic)."
  - "<b>Transport channels</b> say how it is carried: BCH, PCH, DL-SCH, UL-SCH, RACH."
  - "<b>Physical channels</b> are what is on the air. Downlink: PBCH (MIB), PDCCH (control, carries DCI), PDSCH (data, SIBs, paging). Uplink: PRACH (preamble), PUCCH (control, carries UCI), PUSCH (data)."
  - "<b>DCI</b> = downlink control information: grants and assignments. <b>UCI</b> = uplink control information: HARQ ACK/NACK, scheduling request, channel state (CQI/PMI/RI)."
  - LTE also has PCFICH and PHICH. NR removed both.
say: PDCCH tells the phone where its data is. PDSCH and PUSCH carry the data. PUCCH carries ACKs, scheduling requests and channel reports.
trap: SIBs are sent on PDSCH, not on PBCH. Only the MIB is on PBCH.
