title: NSA and SA, bearers
source: v1
points:
  - <b>NSA (EN-DC, option 3x):</b> the phone is anchored on LTE (master node, MCG) and NR is added as a secondary node (SCG). The core is still the EPC.
  - <b>SA (option 2):</b> NR only, with the 5G core. Registration instead of attach, PDU session instead of PDN connection.
  - <b>SRB</b> = signaling radio bearer. SRB0 carries the first RRC messages on the common channel. SRB1 carries RRC (and early NAS). SRB2 carries NAS after security is on. SRB3 is the direct NR SRB in EN-DC.
  - "<b>DRB</b> = data radio bearer. LTE: one EPS bearer maps to one DRB. NR: QoS flows map to DRBs through SDAP."
  - "QoS class: QCI in LTE (for example QCI 9 default internet, QCI 1 voice, QCI 5 IMS signaling). 5QI in 5G."
say: NSA adds an NR leg to an LTE anchor on the EPC. SA is NR with the 5G core end to end.
trap: In NSA the NAS is LTE NAS. You will not see a 5G Registration Request in an NSA log.
