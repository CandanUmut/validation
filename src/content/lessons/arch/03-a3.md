title: NSA and SA, bearers
source: research
points:
- <b>EN-DC (non-standalone):</b> the UE is anchored on LTE (master node, MCG) with NR added as a secondary node (SCG), on the EPC. <i>In practice:</i> often called option 3x.
- '<b>Standalone:</b> NR with the 5G core: registration instead of attach, PDU session instead of PDN connection. <i>In practice:</i> option 2.'
- <b>SRBs:</b> SRB0 for RRC messages on CCCH; SRB1 for RRC (and NAS before SRB2 exists); SRB2 for NAS and logged measurements, after security; SRB3 for some RRC messages in EN-DC or NR-DC; SRB4 for application-layer measurement reports.
- <b>DRBs:</b> LTE maps one EPS bearer to one DRB; NR maps QoS flows to DRBs through SDAP.
- '<b>5QI</b> examples: 1 conversational voice, 5 IMS signalling, 9 buffered TCP traffic such as web. LTE uses QCI.'
say: EN-DC adds an NR leg to an LTE anchor on the EPC. Standalone is NR with the 5G core end to end.
trap: 'In EN-DC the NAS is LTE NAS: you will not see a 5G Registration Request in that log.'
