title: What to read in a UE log
source: practice
points:
- <i>In practice:</i> <b>serving cell:</b> band, channel (NR-ARFCN/EARFCN), PCI, SS-RSRP, SS-RSRQ, SS-SINR over time.
- '<i>In practice:</i> <b>over-the-air messages:</b> RRC and NAS messages with timestamps: the backbone of every timeline.'
- <i>In practice:</i> <b>RACH:</b> attempts, preamble power, RAR received or not, timing advance, Msg3/Msg4 outcome.
- <i>In practice:</i> <b>downlink:</b> MCS, RBs, rank, CQI, BLER, HARQ retransmissions; <b>uplink:</b> grants, transmit power, power headroom, BSR, uplink BLER.
- <i>In practice:</i> <b>layer 2:</b> RLC retransmissions and status reports, PDCP statistics.
- <i>In practice:</i> <b>events:</b> radio link failure, re-establishment, handover start and end, timer expiries.
- <i>In practice:</i> vendor tools label these as numbered log packets; check the IDs in the vendor's documentation before you quote them.
say: I build a timeline from the RRC and NAS messages, then lay the radio measurements and MAC statistics beside it.
trap: Name the measurement exactly (SS-RSRP or CSI-RSRP, first-transmission or residual BLER). Ambiguous numbers waste a debugging session.
