title: What to read in a UE log
source: v1
points:
  - <b>Serving cell:</b> band, channel number, PCI, RSRP, RSRQ, SINR, over time.
  - <b>Over-the-air messages:</b> RRC and NAS messages with timestamps. This is the backbone of every timeline.
  - <b>RACH:</b> attempts, preamble power, RAR received or not, timing advance value, Msg3 and Msg4 result.
  - <b>Downlink:</b> MCS, number of RBs, rank, CQI reported, BLER, HARQ retransmissions.
  - <b>Uplink:</b> grants, transmit power, power headroom, BSR, uplink BLER.
  - <b>Layer 2:</b> RLC retransmissions, status reports, PDCP statistics.
  - <b>Events:</b> radio link failure, re-establishment, handover start and end, timer expiries.
  - In QXDM these appear as numbered log packets, for example LTE RRC OTA (0xB0C0) and NR RRC OTA (0xB821). Check exact IDs before you quote them.
say: I build a timeline from the RRC and NAS messages, then lay the radio measurements and MAC statistics next to it.
