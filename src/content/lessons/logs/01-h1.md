title: The tool map

points:
- <i>In practice:</i> each modem vendor has its own diagnostic tool, for example Qualcomm QXDM (live) and QCAT (offline analysis); Samsung and MediaTek have their own.
- <i>In practice:</i> drive-test suites (for example Accuver XCAL, Keysight Nemo, Rohde &amp; Schwarz QualiPoc) record UE logs with GPS and run scripted tests.
- <i>In practice:</i> Wireshark decodes network-side protocols such as NGAP, S1AP and GTP-U.
- <i>In practice:</i> network-side logs show the same messages from the other end, plus what the scheduler decided.
- <i>In practice:</i> hardware debuggers (for example Lauterbach TRACE32 over JTAG) are for firmware, below protocol logs.
- 'Whatever the tool, the content is defined by 3GPP: RRC and NAS messages (ASN.1 in TS 38.331 and the NAS specs), MAC and PHY statistics, and NGAP/XnAP on the network side.'
say: Every vendor has its own tool, but they all show the same 3GPP layers. I read the layers and match them to the network-side log.
trap: Be exact about which tools you have used. Knowing one well transfers; pretending to know another does not. Tool names here are industry knowledge, not from a spec.
