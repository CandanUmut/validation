points:
  - "<b>QXDM</b> (Qualcomm): real-time diagnostic logging from Qualcomm modems. <b>QCAT</b> decodes and analyzes the saved logs offline."
  - "<b>XCAL</b> (Accuver): drive-test collection tool. It drives the phones, records modem logs plus GPS, and runs test scripts. <b>XCAP</b> is its post-processing tool."
  - "<b>Shannon DM</b> (Samsung): the same role as QXDM for Samsung Exynos modems."
  - "Other names you may hear: MediaTek ELT, Keysight Nemo, Rohde &amp; Schwarz QualiPoc, Wireshark for S1AP, NGAP and GTP."
  - <b>Network-side logs</b> (your Keysight protocol logs) show the same messages from the other end, plus what the scheduler decided.
  - <b>TRACE32</b> is a hardware debugger (JTAG). It is for firmware-level debugging, a deeper level than protocol logs.
  - The skill that transfers is reading the layers, not the tool's menus.
say: "Every modem vendor has its own log tool, but they all show the same things: over-the-air messages, layer 1 and MAC statistics, and events. I read the layers, and match them to the network-side log."
trap: Be exact about which tools you have used. Knowing one vendor tool well transfers. Pretending to know another does not.
