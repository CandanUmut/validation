title: A debugging method you can say out loud
source: research
points:
- <i>In practice:</i> 1. <b>State the symptom exactly:</b> what failed, how often, since which build, on which phones.
- <i>In practice:</i> 2. <b>Reproduce</b> it, and find the smallest setup that still fails.
- <i>In practice:</i> 3. <b>Build a timeline:</b> align UE, network and test-script logs on one clock.
- <i>In practice:</i> 4. <b>Find the last good message</b> and the first wrong or missing one.
- <i>In practice:</i> 5. <b>Pick the layer:</b> sent but not received → go down; received and rejected → go up.
- '<i>In practice:</i> 6. <b>Compare</b> with a passing run: other build, other phone, other config.'
- <i>In practice:</i> 7. <b>One hypothesis, one change</b> to test it.
- <i>In practice:</i> 8. <b>Classify:</b> UE, base station, core, configuration, RF setup, or the test itself.
- <i>In practice:</i> 9. <b>Report:</b> evidence, root cause or best narrowing, and a regression test.
say: Symptom, reproduce, timeline, last good message, pick the layer, compare with a pass, test one hypothesis, report with evidence.
trap: Jumping to a guess before building the timeline is the most common mistake under interview pressure. Say 'first I would build the timeline'.
