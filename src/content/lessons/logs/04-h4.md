points:
  - "When the base station is the product, you also read its own logs: MAC scheduler traces, PHY measurements (uplink SINR, timing offset, frequency offset), RRC and S1AP/NGAP traces, counters."
  - "The base station sees what the phone cannot: uplink quality, why the scheduler did not grant, whether a preamble was detected, what the core answered."
  - "The phone sees what the base station cannot: downlink quality, whether it decoded the DCI, its own power and timers."
  - Root cause often needs both views. A message 'sent' on one side and 'never received' on the other localizes the fault to the air link or the layers below.
  - "Core side: S1AP/NGAP messages such as Initial UE Message, Initial Context Setup, UE Context Release with cause."
say: The two logs are two halves of one story. Where they disagree is usually where the bug is.
