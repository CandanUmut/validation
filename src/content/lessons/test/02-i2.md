title: What a test plan contains
source: practice
points:
- <i>In practice:</i> <b>scope:</b> the feature, and what is in and out.
- <i>In practice:</i> <b>references:</b> the requirement and the 3GPP clause it comes from (spec, section, release).
- <i>In practice:</i> <b>environment:</b> build, phones, RF setup, core, tools.
- <i>In practice:</i> <b>test cases:</b> ID, purpose, preconditions, configuration, steps, expected result, pass/fail criteria, KPIs to record.
- <i>In practice:</i> <b>coverage matrix:</b> bands, bandwidths, phone models, signal conditions, mobility cases.
- <i>In practice:</i> <b>entry and exit criteria</b>, priorities, risks, schedule.
- <i>In practice:</i> <b>results tracking:</b> where results go, how failures are triaged, who signs off.
say: A test plan ties each test to a requirement, defines pass and fail before the run, and says how results reach sign-off.
trap: Pass/fail criteria written after the run are not criteria. Define them first.
