title: What a test plan contains
source: v1
points:
  - "Scope: the feature, what is in and out."
  - "References: the requirement and the 3GPP clause it comes from."
  - "Test environment: build, phones, RF setup, core, tools."
  - "Test cases: ID, purpose, preconditions, configuration, steps, expected result, pass/fail criteria, KPIs to record."
  - "Coverage matrix: bands, bandwidths, phone models, signal conditions, mobility cases."
  - Entry and exit criteria, priorities, risks, schedule.
  - "Results tracking: where results go, how failures are triaged, who signs off."
say: A test plan ties each test to a requirement, defines pass and fail before the run, and says how results are tracked to sign-off.
trap: Pass/fail criteria written after the run are not criteria. Define them first.
