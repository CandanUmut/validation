title: Framework design
source: v1
points:
  - <b>Layers:</b> equipment drivers at the bottom, a test library in the middle, test cases on top, an orchestrator beside them, a results database and reports at the end.
  - <b>Drivers:</b> instruments over SCPI on LAN, programmable attenuators, phones over ADB or AT commands, power over PDU, consoles over UART.
  - <b>Reliability:</b> timeouts on every wait, bounded retries, state recovery, health checks before and after, clean teardown even on failure.
  - <b>Evidence:</b> on failure, automatically save phone logs, network logs and the config used.
  - <b>pytest tools:</b> fixtures with scopes for setup and teardown, <code>parametrize</code> for the coverage matrix, markers to select suites, <code>conftest.py</code> for shared fixtures, xdist for parallel runs, hooks for custom reports.
  - <b>Parallelism:</b> one worker per device or per RF chain. Shared equipment needs a lock.
say: Drivers, library, tests, orchestrator, results. Every wait has a timeout, every failure saves evidence, every test cleans up after itself.
