points:
- <i>In practice:</i> <b>layers:</b> equipment drivers, a test library, test cases, an orchestrator, and a results database with reports.
- <i>In practice:</i> <b>drivers:</b> instruments over SCPI on LAN, programmable attenuators, phones over ADB or AT commands, power over a PDU, consoles over UART.
- <i>In practice:</i> <b>reliability:</b> a timeout on every wait, bounded retries, state recovery, health checks before and after, clean teardown even on failure.
- <i>In practice:</i> <b>evidence:</b> on failure, automatically save UE logs, network logs and the config used.
- <i>In practice:</i> <b>pytest:</b> scoped fixtures for setup and teardown, <code>parametrize</code> for the coverage matrix, markers to pick suites, <code>conftest.py</code> for shared fixtures, xdist for parallel runs, hooks for reports.
- <i>In practice:</i> <b>parallelism:</b> one worker per device or RF chain; shared equipment needs a lock.
say: Drivers, library, tests, orchestrator, results. Every wait has a timeout, every failure saves evidence, every test cleans up after itself.
