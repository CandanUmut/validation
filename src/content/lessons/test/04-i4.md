title: Flaky tests in an RF lab
source: v1
points:
  - "Common causes: RF leakage, loose connectors, shared equipment, phone left in a bad state by the previous test, timing races in the script, network state not reset (forbidden lists, back-off timers)."
  - "Decide first: is it the test, the setup, or the product? A real intermittent product bug looks the same as a flaky test."
  - "Tools: rerun in isolation, rerun on another device, compare logs of pass and fail, add health checks, add state reset."
  - "For radio behavior, a statistical pass rule is honest: for example at least 98 of 100 attach attempts succeed."
  - Quarantine a flaky test with a ticket and an owner. Do not delete it and do not let it block everything.
say: I treat an intermittent failure as real until I can show it is the test. Then I fix the test, not hide it.
trap: Blind retries hide real intermittent bugs. Retry only what you can explain, and log every retry.
