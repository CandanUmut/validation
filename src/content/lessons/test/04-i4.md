title: Flaky tests in an RF lab
source: practice
points:
- '<i>In practice:</i> common causes: RF leakage, loose connectors, shared equipment, a phone left in a bad state by the previous test, timing races in the script, network state not reset.'
- 'State that outlives a test is real and specified: the forbidden PLMNs list is on the SIM and survives switch-off; forbidden tracking-area lists last until switch-off or 12–24 hours; NAS back-off timers such as T3402 (12 min default) keep running.'
- '<i>In practice:</i> decide first: is it the test, the setup, or the product? A real intermittent product bug looks the same as a flaky test.'
- '<i>In practice:</i> tools: rerun in isolation, rerun on another device, compare pass and fail logs, add health checks and state resets.'
- <i>In practice:</i> for radio behaviour a statistical pass rule is honest, for example at least 98 of 100 attempts succeed.
- <i>In practice:</i> quarantine a flaky test with a ticket and an owner; do not delete it, and do not let it block everything.
say: I treat an intermittent failure as real until I can show it is the test. Then I fix the test, not hide it.
trap: Blind retries hide real intermittent bugs. Retry only what you can explain, and log every retry.
