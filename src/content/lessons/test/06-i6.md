  - "Your experience: phone or modem is the DUT, a network emulator is the known-good partner."
  - "This job: eNB/gNB software is the DUT, phones are the known-good partner. The messages are the same. The pass/fail question moves to the other side."
  - "New test tools you would meet: real phones in shield boxes, UE simulators that act as many phones at once, channel emulators that add fading, delay and Doppler, and a core network (real or simulated)."
  - "New kinds of tests: capacity with many users, scheduler fairness, admission control, counters and KPIs correctness, behavior with misbehaving phones."
  - "What stays the same: the framework, device control, log capture, triage, dashboards, CI."
say: It is the same conversation tested from the other side. My automation and debugging carry over, and the base station internals are what I need to learn fastest.
trap: Do not say 'it is the same thing'. Say what is the same and what is new.
