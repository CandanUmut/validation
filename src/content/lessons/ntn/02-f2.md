title: 'Two ways to do it: NTN-aware phones or unmodified phones'
source: research
points:
- <b>3GPP NTN (Release 17):</b> the UE is GNSS-capable, reads the satellite ephemeris and Common TA from SIB19, pre-compensates its timing and its uplink Doppler, and does not transmit at all without a valid GNSS fix, ephemeris and Common TA.
- <b>Direct-to-cell with unmodified LTE phones:</b> the phone believes it sees a normal cell, so every correction has to happen on the network side.
- <i>Reasoning, not a spec:</i> the network can pre-shift downlink frequency and timing so they are right at the beam centre; each phone then only sees the residual error for its spot in the beam.
- <i>Reasoning, not a spec:</i> smaller beams leave smaller residual Doppler and delay spread across the beam, at the cost of more beams and more handovers.
- '<i>Reasoning, not a spec:</i> with unmodified phones the margins are thin, so validation must find the edges: elevation, beam edge, phone model.'
say: With NTN-aware phones, the phone compensates. With unmodified phones, the satellite must make itself look like a normal cell and only a residual error is left.
trap: Vendors' and operators' internal designs are not public. Label your reasoning as reasoning; interviewers respect that more than a confident guess.
