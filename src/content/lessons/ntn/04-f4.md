title: What breaks, layer by layer
source: v1
points:
  - <b>Sync:</b> uncorrected Doppler puts PSS/SSS outside the phone's search range. The phone never sees the cell.
  - <b>PHY:</b> residual frequency offset causes inter-carrier interference. Low SINR forces low MCS. Doppler rate stresses the phone's frequency tracking loop.
  - <b>PRACH:</b> timing uncertainty across a wide beam can exceed what the preamble format tolerates. The base station must search a wider window.
  - <b>Timing advance:</b> the absolute delay is beyond the normal TA range, and it drifts quickly, so TA commands must be frequent or the timeline must be shifted on the network side.
  - <b>MAC:</b> the RAR window, contention timer and fixed HARQ timing (n+4 in LTE FDD) all assume a short round trip.
  - <b>RLC / PDCP:</b> reordering and poll timers set for terrestrial HARQ delay fire too early.
  - <b>RRC:</b> every phone in a beam must hand over when the satellite moves on, about the same time. Radio link failure timers must tolerate short gaps.
  - <b>NAS / core:</b> PLMN selection and roaming behavior decide when a phone goes to the satellite at all. Tracking areas must make sense while cells sweep across the ground.
