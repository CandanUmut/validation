title: Release 17 NR-NTN feature list
source: research
points:
- <b>SIB19:</b> ephemeris, epoch time, Common TA parameters, <code>ntn-UlSyncValidityDuration</code>, <code>cellSpecificKoffset</code>, <code>kmac</code>, <code>t-Service</code> (when the cell stops), reference location, neighbour satellite information.
- '<b>Timing advance:</b> the UE computes its own part from its GNSS position and the ephemeris, and adds the <b>Common TA</b>: the round-trip time between the uplink reference point and the satellite.'
- <b>K_offset:</b> a scheduling offset at least the service-link RTT plus the Common TA, added to uplink timing relationships so the large timing advance fits; <code>k_mac</code> delays when MAC CE commands take effect.
- <b>HARQ:</b> up to 32 processes. Downlink feedback can be disabled per process; uplink processes can be set to HARQ mode A or B.
- <b>Timers:</b> the RAR window starts later (by T_TA + k_mac); contention resolution and DRX HARQ RTT timers add the UE–gNB round trip; longer values for <code>sr-ProhibitTimer</code> (up to 1082 ms), RLC <code>t-Reassembly</code> and the PDCP discard timer (2000 ms).
- <b>Mobility:</b> earth-fixed, quasi-earth-fixed and earth-moving cells; conditional handover with <b>time-based</b> and <b>location-based</b> triggers, always together with a measurement trigger (A3, A4 or A5).
- '<b>Scope:</b> transparent payload. Bands <b>n256</b> (S-band: UL 1980–2010, DL 2170–2200 MHz) and <b>n255</b> (L-band: UL 1626.5–1660.5, DL 1525–1559 MHz). E-UTRA IoT-NTN uses SIB31.'
say: Release 17 gives the UE the satellite's orbit, lets it pre-correct time and frequency, stretches timing with K_offset, and relaxes HARQ and timers.
trap: 'Release 17 NTN needs GNSS in the UE: without a valid fix it does not transmit. A legacy phone does none of this, so the two approaches are tested differently.'
