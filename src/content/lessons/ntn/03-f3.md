title: Release 17 NR-NTN feature list
source: v1
points:
  - <b>SIB19:</b> satellite ephemeris, common timing advance parameters, epoch time, validity duration (<code>ntn-UlSyncValidityDuration</code>), <code>cellSpecificKoffset</code>, <code>kMac</code>, stop-serving time, reference location.
  - <b>Timing advance:</b> the phone computes its own part from GNSS position and ephemeris, and adds the common TA broadcast by the network (for the feeder link).
  - <b>Frequency:</b> the phone pre-compensates uplink Doppler on the service link.
  - <b>K_offset:</b> an extra scheduling offset added to uplink timings (PUSCH after a grant, HARQ-ACK, Msg3) so the large timing advance still fits.
  - <b>HARQ:</b> up to 32 processes, and feedback can be disabled per process. Then reliability comes from repetitions and RLC.
  - <b>Timers extended:</b> random access response window, contention resolution timer, SR prohibit timer, DRX HARQ timers, RLC reassembly, PDCP discard.
  - <b>Mobility:</b> earth-fixed, quasi-earth-fixed and earth-moving cells. Conditional handover with time-based and location-based triggers. Neighbor satellite info for measurement timing.
  - <b>Scope:</b> transparent payload, FR1, bands n255 (L-band) and n256 (S-band). Release 17 also defined IoT-NTN for NB-IoT and LTE-M.
  - <b>Where to read:</b> TR 38.811 and TR 38.821 (studies), TS 38.300 clause 16.14 (overview), TS 38.331 (RRC, SIB19), TS 38.213 (timing), TS 38.101-5 (UE RF for NTN).
say: Release 17 gives the phone the satellite's orbit, lets it pre-correct time and frequency, stretches the timing relationships with K_offset, and relaxes HARQ and timers.
trap: Release 17 NTN needs GNSS in the phone. An unmodified legacy phone does none of this, which is why the two approaches are tested differently.
