title: What a validation engineer would test
source: v1
points:
  - <b>Access:</b> time from no service to registered, RACH success rate versus elevation angle, attach success across phone models.
  - <b>Link quality:</b> residual frequency offset, timing advance distribution, BLER and MCS versus elevation, uplink power headroom.
  - <b>Mobility:</b> handover success during beam and satellite changes, drop rate over a full pass, recovery time after a gap.
  - <b>Services:</b> SMS delivery time and success, data session setup, throughput per beam, voice quality where offered.
  - <b>Interoperability:</b> many phone models and modem chipsets (Qualcomm, MediaTek, Samsung, Apple), because each tolerates Doppler and timing differently.
  - <b>Coexistence:</b> the phone should prefer the terrestrial network and return to it quickly. Test the edges of coverage.
  - <b>Regression:</b> every new eNB/gNB software build runs the same automated suite, and KPIs are compared build over build.
  - <b>Lab first:</b> a channel emulator adds satellite delay and Doppler profiles so a pass can be replayed on the bench. Then confirm in the field under real satellites.
say: I would test access, link quality, mobility and services across many phone models, first with emulated passes in the lab and then under real satellites.
