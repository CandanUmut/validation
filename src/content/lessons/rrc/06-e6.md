points:
  - <b>T300:</b> RRC connection setup. <b>T301:</b> re-establishment request. <b>T304:</b> handover. <b>T311:</b> cell search after failure.
  - "<b>N310 / T310 / N311:</b> after N310 out-of-sync indications, T310 starts. N311 in-sync indications stop it. If T310 expires: radio link failure."
  - <b>T3410</b> (LTE attach, 15 s), <b>T3411</b> (retry after 10 s), <b>T3402</b> (12 minutes after 5 failed attempts), <b>T3412</b> (periodic tracking area update).
  - "5G: <b>T3510</b> (registration, 15 s), <b>T3511</b> (10 s), <b>T3502</b> (12 minutes), <b>T3512</b> (periodic registration)."
  - Each timer is a trade-off. Short T310 drops calls on brief fades. Long T310 keeps the phone stuck on a dead link.
say: T310 with N310 and N311 decides when a bad link becomes a failure. T300 and T304 guard setup and handover.
trap: If a test shows a retry every 10 seconds five times and then silence for 12 minutes, that is T3411 and T3402 working as designed.
