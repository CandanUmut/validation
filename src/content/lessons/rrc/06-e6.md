title: Timers and counters
source: research
points:
- '<b>RRC (NR):</b> <b>T300</b> setup (starts when RRCSetupRequest is sent), <b>T301</b> re-establishment request, <b>T304</b> handover (reconfiguration with sync), <b>T311</b> cell search during re-establishment (on expiry: go to idle), <b>T319</b> resume.'
- <b>N310 / T310 / N311:</b> after N310 consecutive out-of-sync indications, T310 starts; N311 consecutive in-sync indications stop it; if T310 expires, radio link failure.
- 'NR ranges from SIB1: <code>t300</code> 100–2000 ms, <code>t310</code> 0–2000 ms, <code>n310</code> 1–20, <code>t311</code> 1–30 s, <code>n311</code> 1–10.'
- <b>LTE NAS:</b> <b>T3410</b> 15 s (attach), <b>T3411</b> 10 s (retry), <b>T3402</b> default 12 min (started when the attach attempt counter reaches 5), <b>T3412</b> default 54 min (periodic TAU).
- <b>5G NAS:</b> <b>T3510</b> 15 s (registration), <b>T3511</b> 10 s, <b>T3502</b> default 12 min (attempt counter reaches 5), <b>T3512</b> default 54 min (periodic registration).
- 'Each value is a trade-off: a short T310 drops calls on brief fades; a long one keeps the UE stuck on a dead link.'
say: 'T310 with N310 and N311 decides when a bad link becomes a failure. T300, T301 and T304 guard setup, re-establishment and handover. NAS: 15 s, 10 s, 12 min, 54 min.'
trap: A UE that waits exactly 12 minutes after a core outage is probably in T3402 or T3502 back-off after five failed attempts, not broken.
