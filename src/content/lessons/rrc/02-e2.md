title: System information
source: v1
points:
  - <b>MIB:</b> the minimum to find SIB1.
  - <b>SIB1:</b> PLMN list, tracking area code, cell identity, barring, minimum signal level (<code>q-RxLevMin</code>), and the schedule of other SIBs. In NR it also carries the common serving cell config, including RACH.
  - "<b>LTE SIB2:</b> common radio config: RACH, PRACH, PUCCH, PUSCH, power control, timers (T300, T310 and so on)."
  - <b>SIB3 to SIB5:</b> cell reselection rules and neighbors.
  - <b>NTN:</b> NR SIB19 carries satellite ephemeris, common timing advance and timing offsets. LTE IoT-NTN uses SIB31.
  - When system information changes, phones are told through paging or a value tag.
say: SIB1 answers 'may I camp here and how do I access'. A lot of RAN parameter tuning is really SIB content.
