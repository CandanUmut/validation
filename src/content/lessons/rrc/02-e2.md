points:
- '<b>MIB</b> (on PBCH) gives just enough to find SIB1: system frame number, subcarrier spacing, where the SIB1 control channel is (<code>pdcch-ConfigSIB1</code>). In NR it also carries <code>cellBarred</code> and <code>intraFreqReselection</code>.'
- '<b>SIB1</b> answers ''may I camp here and how do I access'': PLMN list, tracking area code, RAN area code, cell identity, <code>q-RxLevMin</code> for cell selection, the schedule of the other SIBs, and the common serving cell configuration (including random access).'
- In NR, SIB1 also carries the UE timers (<code>t300</code>, <code>t301</code>, <code>t310</code>, <code>n310</code>, <code>t311</code>, <code>n311</code>, <code>t319</code>) and unified access control barring (<code>uac-BarringInfo</code>). LTE carries the timers in SIB2.
- 'Cell barring differs by RAT: NR puts <code>cellBarred</code> in the <b>MIB</b>; LTE puts it in <b>SIB1</b>.'
- <b>NR reselection SIBs:</b> SIB2 common reselection info, SIB3 intra-frequency neighbours, SIB4 inter-frequency, SIB5 E-UTRA neighbours.
- '<b>NTN:</b> NR SIB19 carries satellite assistance: ephemeris, epoch time, common timing advance (<code>ta-Info</code>), <code>cellSpecificKoffset</code>, <code>kmac</code>, and how long the UL sync stays valid. LTE IoT-NTN uses SIB31.'
- <b>Changes:</b> in NR the UE learns that SI changed from a Short Message with P-RNTI on DCI; the new SI applies from the next modification period.
say: MIB finds SIB1; SIB1 says whether and how to access; the other SIBs drive reselection. In NR, barring is in the MIB and the timers are in SIB1.
trap: 'Looking for the barring bit or T300 in the wrong place is a common NR log-reading mistake: <code>cellBarred</code> is in the MIB, timers are in SIB1, not SIB2.'
