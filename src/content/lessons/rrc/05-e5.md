title: Reject causes worth knowing
source: v1
points:
  - "<b>#3</b> Illegal UE, <b>#6</b> Illegal ME: authentication or equipment identity failed."
  - "<b>#7</b> EPS (or 5GS) services not allowed: subscription has no LTE or 5G service."
  - <b>#11</b> PLMN not allowed, <b>#12</b> Tracking area not allowed, <b>#13</b> Roaming not allowed in this tracking area, <b>#15</b> No suitable cells in tracking area.
  - <b>#17</b> Network failure, <b>#19</b> ESM failure (look inside for the ESM cause), <b>#22</b> Congestion (with a back-off timer).
  - "ESM / 5GSM: <b>#27</b> Missing or unknown APN/DNN, <b>#33</b> Requested service option not subscribed."
  - "5G only: <b>#27</b> (5GMM) N1 mode not allowed, <b>#62</b> No network slices available."
  - Some causes put the PLMN or tracking area on a forbidden list, so the phone will not retry until reboot or timer. A test must clear that state.
say: "I read the cause, then ask: is it subscription, roaming rules, network failure, or congestion?"
trap: "After cause #11 the phone may refuse to try that PLMN again. A later 'failure' may be the phone obeying the forbidden list."
