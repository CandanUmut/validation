points:
- '<b>Both LTE (EMM) and 5G (5GMM):</b> #3 Illegal UE, #6 Illegal ME, #7 EPS services (5GS services) not allowed, #11 PLMN not allowed, #12 Tracking area not allowed, #13 Roaming not allowed in this tracking area, #15 No suitable cells in tracking area, #22 Congestion.'
- '<b>LTE only:</b> #17 Network failure, #19 ESM failure (look inside for the ESM cause). These numbers do not exist as 5GMM causes.'
- '<b>5G only:</b> #20 MAC failure, #21 Synch failure, #27 N1 mode not allowed, #31 Redirection to EPC required, #62 No network slices available, #65 Maximum number of PDU sessions reached.'
- '<b>Session causes:</b> #27 Missing or unknown APN (LTE) / DNN (5G); #33 Requested service option not subscribed.'
- <b>#11</b> puts the PLMN on the <b>forbidden PLMNs list on the SIM</b>. That list survives switch-off and SIM removal. The home PLMN is never put on it. It is cleared by a later successful manual selection, or when <b>T3245</b> expires if the UE uses it.
- 'Forbidden <b>tracking area</b> lists (#12, #13, #15) are erased at switch-off, SIM removal, and periodically every 12 to 24 hours.'
say: 'Read the cause, then ask: identity, subscription, roaming rules, congestion, or a 5G-only reason like slices or N1 mode? And check what list it wrote to.'
trap: 'After a #11 test, an airplane-mode cycle does not clear the forbidden PLMN list: it lives on the SIM. Clear it with your SIM tool or use a test PLMN that is the SIM''s home PLMN.'
