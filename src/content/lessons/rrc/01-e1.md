title: RRC states
source: v1
points:
  - <b>LTE:</b> RRC_IDLE and RRC_CONNECTED.
  - <b>NR:</b> RRC_IDLE, RRC_CONNECTED and <b>RRC_INACTIVE</b>. In inactive, the context is kept so the phone can resume quickly.
  - "Idle: the phone picks cells by itself (reselection), listens for paging, and has no radio bearers."
  - "Connected: the network controls mobility (handover), and the phone reports measurements."
  - An <b>inactivity timer</b> in the base station releases idle connections. Short timer saves resources but causes more signaling.
say: Idle means the phone manages itself. Connected means the network manages it. NR adds inactive as a fast-resume middle state.
