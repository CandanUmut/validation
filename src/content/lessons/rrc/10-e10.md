module: rrc

title: "Idle mode: selection, reselection, paging"
source: v1
points:
  - "<b>Cell selection:</b> the cell must pass the S criterion: <code>Srxlev = measured RSRP - (q-RxLevMin + offset) - Pcompensation > 0</code>."
  - <b>Reselection:</b> by frequency priority first, then by ranking (R criterion) with hysteresis.
  - <b>PLMN selection:</b> home and preferred PLMNs first. This decides when a phone uses a roaming or satellite PLMN.
  - <b>Paging:</b> the phone wakes at its paging occasion, once per DRX cycle (for example 1.28 s), and checks for P-RNTI.
  - A paged phone answers with random access and a Service Request.
say: The phone camps where the S criterion passes, on the highest priority layer, and wakes once per paging cycle.
trap: If <code>q-RxLevMin</code> is set too low, phones camp on a cell they cannot actually reach in the uplink. Camping succeeds, RACH fails.
