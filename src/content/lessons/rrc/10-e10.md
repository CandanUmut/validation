title: 'Idle mode: selection, reselection, paging'
source: research
points:
- <b>Cell selection (NR):</b> the S criterion needs <b>both</b> <code>Srxlev &gt; 0</code> and <code>Squal &gt; 0</code>, where <code>Srxlev = Qrxlevmeas – (Qrxlevmin + Qrxlevminoffset) – Pcompensation – Qoffsettemp</code> and <code>Squal = Qqualmeas – (Qqualmin + Qqualminoffset) – Qoffsettemp</code>.
- <code>Qoffsettemp</code> is the temporary offset from TS 38.331, for example after repeated T300 failures on a cell.
- '<b>Reselection:</b> by frequency priority first; on equal priority, by ranking: <code>Rs = Qmeas,s + Qhyst – Qoffsettemp</code>, <code>Rn = Qmeas,n – Qoffset – Qoffsettemp</code>.'
- <b>Paging:</b> the UE monitors one paging occasion per DRX cycle. The cycle T is the shortest of the RRC-configured UE value, the NAS-configured UE value and the broadcast default (32, 64, 128 or 256 radio frames; 128 frames = 1.28 s).
- <b>Answering paging:</b> core-network paging (5G-S-TMSI) is passed to NAS, which answers with a Service Request. RAN paging of an inactive UE (<code>fullI-RNTI</code>) makes RRC start a <b>resume</b>.
- PLMN selection (TS 23.122) decides which network the UE tries first; the home PLMN is never placed on the forbidden list.
say: Camp where Srxlev and Squal are both positive, reselect by priority then by ranking, wake once per paging cycle, and answer CN paging with NAS but RAN paging with a resume.
trap: A UE that ignores a cell with good RSRP may be failing Squal (RSRQ), or carrying a Qoffsettemp from earlier setup failures.
