title: RRC connection setup
source: research
points:
- 'NR: <code>RRCSetupRequest</code> → <code>RRCSetup</code> → <code>RRCSetupComplete</code>. LTE: <code>RRCConnectionRequest</code> → <code>RRCConnectionSetup</code> → <code>RRCConnectionSetupComplete</code>.'
- 'Setup establishes <b>SRB1</b> and carries the first NAS message: <code>RRCSetupComplete</code> holds <code>dedicatedNAS-Message</code> plus the selected PLMN and, if known, the registered AMF and requested slices.'
- The UE starts <b>T300</b> when it sends the request and stops it on <code>RRCSetup</code> or <code>RRCReject</code>. If T300 expires, RRC tells NAS the setup failed.
- If T300 keeps expiring on the same cell and SIB1 has <code>connEstFailureControl</code>, the UE temporarily applies <code>connEstFailOffset</code> to that cell in cell selection and reselection, so it may move elsewhere.
- '<code>RRCReject</code> may carry <code>waitTime</code> (1 to 16 s): the UE starts <b>T302</b> and stays away for that time.'
- 'Establishment causes in NR: <code>emergency</code>, <code>highPriorityAccess</code>, <code>mt-Access</code>, <code>mo-Signalling</code>, <code>mo-Data</code>, <code>mo-VoiceCall</code>, <code>mo-VideoCall</code>, <code>mo-SMS</code>, <code>mps-PriorityAccess</code>, <code>mcs-PriorityAccess</code>.'
- Next comes AS security with <code>SecurityModeCommand</code>, sent while only SRB1 exists; SRB2 and data bearers come after, in <code>RRCReconfiguration</code>.
- 'If a resume or re-establishment cannot find the UE context, the network may answer with <code>RRCSetup</code> instead: the UE does a fresh setup.'
say: Request, setup, setup complete with NAS inside; then security on SRB1; then reconfiguration adds SRB2 and data bearers. T300 guards the start, T302 enforces a reject's wait time.
trap: 'A resume that ends in <code>RRCSetup</code> is not necessarily a failure: it is the fallback when the context is not found. Count it separately in your KPIs and tests.'
