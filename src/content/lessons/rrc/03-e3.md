---
id: e3
module: rrc
order: 3
title: RRC connection setup
source: v1
points:
  - "LTE: <code>RRCConnectionRequest</code> (Msg3), <code>RRCConnectionSetup</code> (Msg4), <code>RRCConnectionSetupComplete</code> (carries the first NAS message)."
  - "NR: <code>RRCSetupRequest</code>, <code>RRCSetup</code>, <code>RRCSetupComplete</code>."
  - "Then: NAS authentication, NAS security, <code>SecurityModeCommand</code> / <code>SecurityModeComplete</code> at RRC, <code>UECapabilityEnquiry</code> / <code>UECapabilityInformation</code>."
  - Then <code>RRCConnectionReconfiguration</code> (LTE) or <code>RRCReconfiguration</code> (NR) sets up SRB2, data bearers and measurement config.
  - The establishment cause (mo-Signalling, mo-Data, mt-Access, emergency) tells the network why the phone is connecting.
  - <code>RRCConnectionReject</code> / <code>RRCReject</code> with a wait time means the cell is overloaded or admission failed.
say: Request, setup, setup complete with NAS inside, security, capabilities, then reconfiguration to add bearers.
trap: T300 guards the setup. If it expires, the phone never got Msg4. That is a radio or RACH problem, not a NAS problem.
