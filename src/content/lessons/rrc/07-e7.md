title: Measurement events

points:
- <b>A1</b> serving becomes better than a threshold. <b>A2</b> serving becomes worse than a threshold.
- '<b>A3</b> neighbour becomes offset better than the SpCell: the usual handover trigger. Entering condition: <code>Mn + Ofn + Ocn – Hys &gt; Mp + Ofp + Ocp + Off</code>.'
- 'In A3: <code>Ofn</code>/<code>Ofp</code> are the per-frequency <code>offsetMO</code>, <code>Ocn</code>/<code>Ocp</code> the per-cell <code>cellIndividualOffset</code>, <code>Off</code> the <code>a3-Offset</code>, <code>Hys</code> the hysteresis. RSRP in dBm, offsets in dB.'
- <b>A4</b> neighbour better than a threshold. <b>A5</b> SpCell worse than threshold 1 and neighbour better than threshold 2. <b>A6</b> neighbour becomes offset better than an SCell.
- <b>B1</b> inter-RAT neighbour better than a threshold. <b>B2</b> PCell worse than threshold 1 and inter-RAT neighbour better than threshold 2.
- Each event also has <b>time-to-trigger</b>; results are smoothed by the layer 3 <code>filterCoefficient</code>. NTN adds <b>D1</b>, based on distance to a reference location.
- The same A3 definition is used for conditional handover (<b>CondEvent A3</b>).
say: A3 fires when the neighbour, plus its offsets, beats the serving cell plus the offset, by more than the hysteresis, for time-to-trigger.
trap: There are four offsets in A3 (two per side) plus the event offset. When a handover triggers 'too early', check <code>cellIndividualOffset</code> on both cells before blaming <code>a3-Offset</code>.
