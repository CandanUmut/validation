title: Measurement events
source: v1
points:
  - <b>A1:</b> serving better than threshold. <b>A2:</b> serving worse than threshold.
  - <b>A3:</b> neighbor better than serving by an offset. The usual handover trigger.
  - <b>A4:</b> neighbor better than threshold. <b>A5:</b> serving worse than threshold 1 and neighbor better than threshold 2.
  - <b>B1 / B2:</b> the same ideas for a neighbor on another radio technology.
  - Each event has <b>hysteresis</b> and <b>time-to-trigger (TTT)</b>. Results are smoothed by a layer 3 filter coefficient.
  - "Too easy to trigger: early handovers and ping-pong. Too hard: late handovers and drops."
say: A3 is 'neighbor beats serving by an offset for long enough'. Offset, hysteresis and time-to-trigger are the three knobs.
trap: On a moving-satellite cell, signal strength hardly changes between cell center and edge. Strength-based events like A3 work poorly, so time or location triggers are used.
