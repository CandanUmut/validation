title: MAC-level signs of trouble
source: research
points:
- <i>In practice:</i> <b>RACH:</b> many Msg1 with no Msg2 (preamble not heard, or RAR not decoded); Msg3 sent but no Msg4 (contention or uplink quality).
- <i>In practice:</i> <b>Downlink:</b> high first-transmission BLER, many DTX, MCS stuck low, rank stuck at 1.
- <i>In practice:</i> <b>Uplink:</b> BSR keeps growing (not enough grants), PHR at or below zero (power-limited), many uplink retransmissions.
- <b>Sync:</b> if <code>timeAlignmentTimer</code> expires the UE flushes HARQ, releases PUCCH and SRS, and must use random access before sending again.
- <b>Scheduling:</b> when <code>SR_COUNTER</code> reaches <code>sr-TransMax</code>, the UE releases PUCCH and SRS, clears its configured grants and starts random access.
say: 'I read MAC stats as symptoms: BLER and DTX for downlink, BSR and PHR for uplink, RACH counters for access.'
trap: '<i>In practice:</i> a burst of random access in a connected UE is often SR failure or time-alignment expiry, not a new connection. Check which trigger came first.'
