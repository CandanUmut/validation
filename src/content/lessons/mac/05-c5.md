title: MAC-level signs of trouble
source: v1
points:
  - <b>RACH:</b> many Msg1 with no Msg2 (preamble not heard, or RAR not decoded). Msg3 sent but no Msg4 (contention or uplink quality).
  - <b>Downlink:</b> high first-transmission BLER, many DTX, MCS stuck low, rank stuck at 1.
  - <b>Uplink:</b> BSR keeps growing (not enough grants), PHR at or below zero (power-limited), many uplink retransmissions.
  - <b>Sync:</b> timing advance drifting quickly, time alignment timer expiry, then RACH again.
  - <b>Scheduling:</b> SR sent repeatedly without a grant, until <code>sr-TransMax</code> forces a RACH.
say: "I read MAC stats as symptoms: BLER and DTX for downlink, BSR and PHR for uplink, RACH counters for access."
