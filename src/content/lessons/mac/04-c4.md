title: BSR, PHR, MAC control elements, DRX
source: v1
points:
  - "<b>MAC CEs</b> are small control messages inside MAC: timing advance command, BSR, PHR, DRX command, contention resolution identity, SCell activation."
  - <b>BSR</b> reports how much uplink data waits, per logical channel group.
  - <b>PHR</b> reports spare transmit power.
  - "<b>DRX</b> lets the phone sleep. Key timers: <code>onDurationTimer</code>, <code>drx-InactivityTimer</code>, long and short cycle, retransmission timer."
  - "Connected-mode DRX saves battery but adds latency: the network must wait for the next on-duration."
  - Idle-mode DRX is the paging cycle. The phone wakes only at its paging occasion.
say: DRX trades latency for battery. BSR and PHR are how the phone tells the scheduler what it needs and what it can afford.
trap: A test that fails only sometimes with long latency may just be hitting DRX sleep. Check the DRX config before you file a bug.
