title: BSR, PHR, MAC control elements, DRX
source: research
points:
- '<b>MAC CEs</b> are small control messages inside MAC: buffer status report, C-RNTI, UE contention resolution identity, timing advance command, DRX command, configured grant confirmation, power headroom report, SCell activation and more.'
- <b>BSR</b> reports how much uplink data waits, per logical channel group.
- <b>PHR</b> reports the gap between the UE's maximum power and the power its uplink would need.
- '<b>Connected-mode DRX</b> lets the UE sleep. NR timers: <code>drx-onDurationTimer</code>, <code>drx-InactivityTimer</code>, <code>drx-LongCycleStartOffset</code>, <code>drx-ShortCycle</code>, <code>drx-RetransmissionTimerDL</code>/<code>UL</code>.'
- 'DRX saves battery but adds latency: new downlink data waits for the next on-duration.'
- 'Idle-mode DRX is the paging cycle: the UE wakes only at its paging occasion.'
say: DRX trades latency for battery. BSR and PHR tell the scheduler what the UE needs and what it can afford.
trap: A test that sometimes shows long latency may just be hitting DRX sleep. Check the DRX config before you file a bug.
