title: HARQ
source: v1
points:
  - <b>HARQ</b> = fast retransmission in MAC with soft combining. The receiver keeps the failed attempt and combines it with the retransmission.
  - It runs as several parallel stop-and-wait <b>processes</b>. LTE FDD has 8 in the downlink. NR allows up to 16 (and up to 32 for NTN in Release 17).
  - <b>NDI</b> (new data indicator) toggles for new data and stays the same for a retransmission.
  - <b>Redundancy versions</b> (RV 0, 2, 3, 1) send different parity bits each time (incremental redundancy).
  - LTE uplink HARQ is synchronous. LTE downlink and all NR HARQ are asynchronous (the DCI names the process).
  - If HARQ gives up after the maximum retransmissions, RLC AM retransmits the data.
  - HARQ assumes a short, known round trip. On a long-delay link the processes run out (HARQ stalling) unless you add processes or switch feedback off.
say: HARQ is the fast repair loop at MAC with soft combining. RLC ARQ is the slower safety net above it.
trap: BLER is counted on first transmissions. A link with 10% BLER and working HARQ loses almost nothing.
