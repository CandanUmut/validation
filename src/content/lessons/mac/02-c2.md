title: Scheduling and grants
source: v1
points:
  - The scheduler in the base station decides, every slot, who gets which RBs and which MCS.
  - "Downlink: DCI on PDCCH, then data on PDSCH. Uplink: DCI grant, then data on PUSCH."
  - "LTE FDD timing is fixed: uplink data 4 ms after the grant, HARQ feedback 4 ms after the data."
  - "NR timing is flexible: <code>K0</code> (DCI to PDSCH), <code>K1</code> (PDSCH to HARQ-ACK), <code>K2</code> (DCI to PUSCH)."
  - "Uplink flow: phone sends a <b>scheduling request (SR)</b> on PUCCH, gets a small grant, sends a <b>buffer status report (BSR)</b>, gets a bigger grant."
  - "Scheduler policies: round robin (fair), max C/I (best throughput), proportional fair (balance). Semi-persistent or configured grants avoid a DCI for every packet (voice)."
say: Downlink is assign-then-send. Uplink is request, grant, report buffer, bigger grant.
trap: "Slow uplink start is often an SR problem: long SR period, SR not detected, or a prohibit timer."
