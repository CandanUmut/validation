title: Data channels, coding and reference signals
source: v1
points:
  - <b>PDSCH</b> and <b>PUSCH</b> carry data. Modulation is QPSK, 16QAM, 64QAM or 256QAM (2, 4, 6, 8 bits per symbol).
  - "Coding: LTE uses turbo codes for data. NR uses <b>LDPC</b> for data and <b>polar</b> codes for control."
  - LTE has <b>CRS</b> (cell-specific reference signal), always on, used for measurement and demodulation.
  - NR has no always-on CRS. It uses <b>DMRS</b> (demodulation, sent with the data), <b>CSI-RS</b> (channel measurement), <b>TRS</b> (tracking), <b>PT-RS</b> (phase tracking, mainly FR2) and <b>SRS</b> (uplink sounding).
  - The transport block size (TBS) comes from MCS, number of RBs and number of layers.
say: LTE measures on always-on CRS. NR measures on SSB and CSI-RS and demodulates with DMRS that travels with the data.
trap: RSRP in LTE is measured on CRS. In NR it is SS-RSRP or CSI-RSRP. Say which one when you quote a number.
