title: "Downlink control: PDCCH and DCI"
source: v1
points:
  - Every scheduled transmission starts with a <b>DCI</b> on PDCCH. The DCI's CRC is scrambled with an RNTI, so only the target phone accepts it.
  - "LTE DCI formats: 0 (uplink grant), 1A/1/2/2A (downlink assignments). NR: 0_0 and 0_1 (uplink), 1_0 and 1_1 (downlink)."
  - NR places PDCCH in a <b>CORESET</b> (the time-frequency area) and a <b>search space</b> (when and where the phone looks).
  - <b>Aggregation level</b> (1, 2, 4, 8, 16 CCEs) is PDCCH's link adaptation. Weak phones need a higher level.
  - "The phone does <b>blind decoding</b>: it tries candidates and keeps the one whose CRC matches."
  - If the phone misses the DCI, it sends nothing back. The network sees <b>DTX</b>, not a NACK.
say: The DCI is the grant. If PDCCH fails, the data never has a chance, and the sign is DTX instead of NACK.
trap: High BLER and many DTX are different problems. NACK means PDSCH failed. DTX points to PDCCH or uplink feedback.
