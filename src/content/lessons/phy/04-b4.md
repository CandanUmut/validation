title: 'Downlink control: PDCCH and DCI'
source: research
points:
- Every scheduled transmission starts with a <b>DCI</b> on PDCCH. Its CRC is scrambled with an RNTI, so only the intended UE accepts it.
- <b>NR DCI formats:</b> 0_0, 0_1, 0_2 (uplink) and 1_0, 1_1, 1_2 (downlink). <b>LTE:</b> 0 (uplink), 1, 1A, 2, 2A and others (downlink).
- NR places PDCCH in a <b>CORESET</b> (where in time and frequency) and a <b>search space</b> (when and which candidates).
- '<b>Aggregation levels</b> 1, 2, 4, 8, 16 CCEs: more CCEs for weaker UEs. The search space sets how many candidates per level.'
- The UE <b>blind-decodes</b> the candidates and keeps the one whose CRC matches its RNTI.
- <i>In practice:</i> if the UE misses the DCI it sends no HARQ feedback; the network sees <b>DTX</b>, not a NACK.
say: The DCI is the grant. If PDCCH fails, the data never had a chance, and the sign is DTX rather than NACK.
trap: 'High BLER and high DTX are different problems: NACK means PDSCH failed; DTX points at PDCCH or the uplink feedback path.'
