title: FDD, TDD and TDD patterns
source: research
points:
- <b>FDD:</b> uplink and downlink on separate paired frequencies, at the same time. <b>TDD:</b> one frequency, taking turns.
- <b>LTE TDD</b> has 7 uplink-downlink configurations (0 to 6) with a special subframe made of DwPTS, GP and UpPTS.
- '<b>NR TDD</b> is set by <code>tdd-UL-DL-ConfigurationCommon</code>: a reference spacing, <code>pattern1</code> and an optional <code>pattern2</code>. Each pattern has a period (0.5 to 10 ms) plus downlink slots and symbols and uplink slots and symbols.'
- <i>In practice:</i> a pattern like DDDSU is common; more downlink slots give more downlink capacity but less uplink and slower feedback.
- <i>In practice:</i> the guard at the downlink-to-uplink switch must cover the round-trip time, so large cells need longer guards.
- <i>In practice:</i> neighbouring TDD cells with different patterns interfere (cross-link interference). NR UEs can measure it with SRS-RSRP and CLI-RSSI.
say: The TDD pattern sets the downlink to uplink split; I check it fits the traffic and that the guard covers the cell range.
trap: Two operators or two cells with misaligned TDD patterns can wreck each other's uplink. Check pattern alignment before chasing an uplink noise problem.
