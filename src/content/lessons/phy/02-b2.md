title: FDD, TDD and TDD patterns
source: v1
points:
  - <b>FDD:</b> uplink and downlink on separate paired frequencies, at the same time.
  - <b>TDD:</b> one frequency, uplink and downlink take turns. A guard period sits at each downlink-to-uplink switch.
  - LTE TDD has 7 fixed uplink-downlink configurations (0 to 6) and a special subframe (DwPTS, GP, UpPTS).
  - "NR TDD is set by <code>tdd-UL-DL-ConfigurationCommon</code>: a period, a number of downlink slots and symbols, and a number of uplink slots and symbols. A common pattern is DDDSU."
  - "Tuning trade-off: more downlink slots give more downlink throughput but less uplink capacity and slower uplink feedback."
  - The guard period must cover the round-trip propagation time. Larger cell or longer path needs a longer guard.
say: The TDD pattern sets the downlink to uplink split. I pick it from the traffic mix and check the guard period fits the cell range.
trap: Two TDD cells with different patterns interfere with each other (cross-link interference). Patterns must be aligned.
