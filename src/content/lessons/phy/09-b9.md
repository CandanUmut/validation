title: MIMO and beams
source: v1
points:
  - <b>Layers</b> are parallel data streams. Rank 2 means two streams. The phone reports the rank it can support (RI).
  - "LTE transmission modes: TM1 single antenna, TM2 transmit diversity, TM3 open-loop spatial multiplexing, TM4 closed-loop."
  - NR uses beams. Each SSB is sent on a beam. The phone picks the best SSB, and that choice sets which PRACH occasion it uses.
  - "Beam management: beam sweeping, measurement, reporting, and <b>beam failure recovery</b> when the serving beam is lost."
  - A satellite phased array forms many spot beams on the ground. Each beam is a cell or part of a cell.
say: Rank is the number of streams. Beams focus energy, and on a satellite the beams are what make the link budget close.
trap: With one phone antenna in bad signal you get rank 1. Do not expect MIMO gains at the edge of coverage.
