title: MIMO and beams
source: research
points:
- <b>Layers</b> are parallel data streams; the UE reports the rank it can take (RI). NR UEs support 2, 4 or 8 downlink layers by capability.
- <b>LTE transmission modes:</b> TM1 single antenna port, TM2 transmit diversity, TM3 large-delay CDD (open-loop spatial multiplexing), TM4 closed-loop spatial multiplexing (and more up to TM10).
- In NR each SSB is sent on a beam. The SSB the UE picks maps to the PRACH occasions it may use (<code>ssb-perRACH-OccasionAndCB-PreamblesPerSSB</code>), so the gNB learns which SSB beam the UE chose.
- '<b>Beam failure detection and recovery</b> (MAC): the UE counts beam-failure instances up to <code>beamFailureInstanceMaxCount</code>, then recovers with random access on a new beam.'
- <i>In practice:</i> a satellite's phased array forms many spot beams; each is a cell or part of one.
say: Rank is the number of streams. Beams focus energy, and the chosen SSB beam decides which PRACH occasion the UE uses.
trap: <i>In practice:</i> with one usable antenna or poor SINR you get rank 1; do not expect MIMO gains at the cell edge.
