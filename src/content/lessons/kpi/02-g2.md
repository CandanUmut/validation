title: Which parameter moves which KPI
source: research
points:
- '<code>q-RxLevMin</code>: who may camp; affects accessibility at the edge.'
- '<b>PRACH</b> (NR name / LTE name): <code>preambleReceivedTargetPower</code> / <code>preambleInitialReceivedTargetPower</code>, <code>powerRampingStep</code>, <code>preambleTransMax</code>, <code>prach-ConfigurationIndex</code> / <code>prach-ConfigIndex</code>, <code>zeroCorrelationZoneConfig</code>. Affect RACH success, access delay and uplink interference.'
- '<b>Uplink power</b>: <code>p0-NominalWithGrant</code> / <code>p0-NominalPUSCH</code>, <code>p0-nominal</code> / <code>p0-NominalPUCCH</code>, <code>alpha</code>. Affect uplink SINR, interference and battery.'
- '<b>Failure timers</b>: <code>t310</code>, <code>n310</code>, <code>n311</code>, <code>t311</code>. Drop rate versus recovery time.'
- '<b>Handover</b>: <code>a3-Offset</code>, <code>hysteresis</code>, <code>timeToTrigger</code>, <code>filterCoefficient</code>, <code>cellIndividualOffset</code>. Handover success versus ping-pong.'
- '<b>DRX and data inactivity</b>: latency and signalling versus battery.'
- '<b>RLC</b>: <code>maxRetxThreshold</code>, <code>t-PollRetransmit</code>, <code>t-Reassembly</code> (NR; LTE also has <code>t-Reordering</code>). Drops and latency.'
- <i>In practice:</i> link adaptation (BLER target, outer-loop steps), TDD pattern and scheduler weights are vendor settings with big effects on throughput and fairness.
say: For each KPI I know two or three parameters that move it, and what each one costs somewhere else.
