title: A sound tuning method
source: v1
points:
  - 1. Write down the goal KPI and the guard KPIs that must not get worse.
  - 2. Record a baseline under fixed conditions (same build, same phones, same RF setup, same traffic).
  - 3. Change one parameter at a time.
  - 4. Collect enough samples to see past random variation. Repeat runs.
  - "5. Compare distributions, not single numbers: median, 10th and 90th percentile."
  - 6. Check the guard KPIs and check real phones, not only the best one.
  - 7. Confirm in a different condition (other phone model, other signal level, field).
  - 8. Record the change, the evidence and how to roll back. Add a regression test.
say: Baseline, one change, enough samples, compare distributions, check side effects, confirm elsewhere, document.
trap: Do not call something an improvement because one number went up on one setup. Check that the setup matches real use.
