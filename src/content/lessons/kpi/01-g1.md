title: KPI families
source: v1
points:
  - <b>Accessibility:</b> can users get in? RACH success rate, RRC setup success rate, bearer or PDU session setup success rate, attach or registration success rate.
  - <b>Retainability:</b> do they stay? Drop rate = abnormal releases / all releases.
  - <b>Mobility:</b> handover success rate (preparation and execution), ping-pong rate.
  - <b>Integrity:</b> how good is it? Throughput, latency, BLER, packet loss.
  - <b>Availability and utilization:</b> cell uptime, RB usage, number of connected users.
  - A KPI is a ratio of counters. Always ask what the numerator and denominator count and over what period.
say: I group KPIs into accessibility, retainability, mobility and integrity, and I always check how each one is counted.
trap: A 99% success rate over 100 attempts and over 100,000 attempts are very different claims. State the sample size.
