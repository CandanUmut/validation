points:
- '<b>3GPP KPI categories.</b> LTE (TS 32.450): accessibility, retainability, integrity, availability, mobility, energy efficiency. 5G (TS 28.554): accessibility, integrity, utilization, retainability, mobility, energy efficiency, reliability.'
- '<b>Accessibility</b>: can users get in? Defined as success ratios, for example successful over attempted registrations; in practice also RACH, RRC setup and bearer or PDU session setup success.'
- '<b>Retainability</b> (3GPP): bearers or QoS flows released abnormally <b>while data was waiting</b>, normalised by <b>session time</b> (5G unit: active releases per second). <i>In practice:</i> many operators also track a per-release drop ratio; say which one you mean.'
- '<b>Mobility</b>: handover success; <i>In practice:</i> split into preparation and execution, plus ping-pong rate.'
- '<b>Integrity</b>: how good it is: IP throughput, latency, packet loss.'
- '<b>Availability / utilization</b>: cell available time; resource usage and connected users.'
- A KPI is built from counters. Always ask what the numerator and denominator count, and over what period.
say: I group KPIs by the 3GPP categories and always check how each one is counted, because two 'drop rates' can be different formulas.
trap: A 99% success rate over 100 attempts and over 100,000 attempts are very different claims. State the sample size.
