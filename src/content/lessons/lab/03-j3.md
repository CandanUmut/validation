title: Conducted and over-the-air testing
source: v1
points:
  - <b>Conducted:</b> cable from the instrument to the phone's antenna port. Repeatable, but it skips the antenna.
  - <b>Over the air (OTA):</b> through antennas, in a shield box or chamber. Includes the antenna, less repeatable.
  - <b>Calibrate path loss</b> for each path and frequency, and store it. Without it, RSRP numbers mean nothing.
  - <b>Leakage:</b> a phone that secretly sees a live network, or a neighbor test bench, gives results that look like random bugs.
  - <b>Level planning:</b> too little attenuation overloads the receiver. Too much hides the signal in noise.
say: Conducted for repeatability, OTA when the antenna matters, and calibration before I trust any level.
