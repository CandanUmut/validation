title: Conducted and over-the-air testing
source: practice
points:
- <i>In practice:</i> <b>conducted:</b> a cable from the instrument to the phone's antenna port. Repeatable, but it skips the antenna.
- <i>In practice:</i> <b>over the air (OTA):</b> through antennas, in a shield box or chamber. Includes the antenna, less repeatable.
- <i>In practice:</i> <b>calibrate path loss</b> for each path and frequency and store it; without it, RSRP numbers mean nothing.
- <i>In practice:</i> <b>leakage:</b> a phone that secretly sees a live network or a neighbouring bench gives results that look like random bugs.
- <i>In practice:</i> <b>level planning:</b> too little attenuation overloads the receiver; too much hides the signal in noise.
say: Conducted for repeatability, OTA when the antenna matters, and calibration before I trust any level.
trap: Re-run the path-loss calibration whenever a cable, splitter or frequency changes.
