points:
  - "<b>Attenuators</b> (fixed and programmable): set path loss and simulate distance or fading."
  - <b>Splitters and combiners:</b> a 2-way splitter loses a bit over 3 dB per branch.
  - <b>Shield boxes:</b> isolate a phone from live networks and from other tests.
  - "<b>Cables and connectors</b> (SMA, N): every one adds loss and can fail. Use the right torque."
  - <b>Circulators, duplexers, filters:</b> separate transmit and receive paths and bands.
  - <b>Channel emulators:</b> add fading, delay and Doppler in a repeatable way.
  - <b>Instruments:</b> spectrum analyzer (what is on the air), signal generator, power meter, vector network analyzer (cable and component loss).
  - <b>Reference clock:</b> instruments share a 10 MHz reference so their frequencies agree.
say: Attenuators set the link, shield boxes keep it clean, and a spectrum analyzer tells me what is really on the cable.
