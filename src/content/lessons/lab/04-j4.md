title: RF troubleshooting checklist
source: practice
points:
- <i>In practice:</i> 1. Is the cell actually on? Check the instrument state and look at the spectrum.
- <i>In practice:</i> 2. Same band, channel and bandwidth on both sides?
- <i>In practice:</i> 3. Connectors tight, right ports, no damaged cable? Swap the cable.
- <i>In practice:</i> 4. Level in range? Check for overload and for too much loss.
- <i>In practice:</i> 5. Path-loss calibration current for this frequency?
- '<i>In practice:</i> 6. Leakage in or out? Switch the cell off: does the phone still see something?'
- <i>In practice:</i> 7. Reference clock locked?
- '<i>In practice:</i> 8. For MIMO: both antenna ports on the right paths?'
- '<i>In practice:</i> 9. Phone in the right mode: band lock, airplane mode off, correct SIM and profile, no leftover forbidden-PLMN entry on the SIM.'
- <i>In practice:</i> 10. Change one thing, retest, write it down.
say: I check that the signal exists, that both sides agree on frequency, that the path and levels are right, and that nothing leaks.
trap: If the phone sees the cell but never tries to register, check the SIM's forbidden PLMNs list before you touch the RF.
