title: RF troubleshooting checklist
source: v1
points:
  - 1. Is the cell actually on? Check the instrument state and look at the spectrum.
  - 2. Right band, channel number and bandwidth on both sides?
  - 3. Connectors tight, right ports, no damaged cable? Swap the cable.
  - 4. Is the level in range? Check for overload and for too much loss.
  - 5. Is the path loss calibration current for this frequency?
  - "6. Any leakage in or out? Check with the cell switched off: does the phone still see something?"
  - 7. Reference clock locked?
  - "8. For MIMO: are both antenna ports connected to the right paths?"
  - 9. Is the phone in the right mode (band lock, airplane mode off, correct SIM and profile)?
  - 10. Change one thing, retest, write it down.
say: I check that the signal exists, that both sides agree on frequency, that the path and levels are right, and that nothing leaks.
