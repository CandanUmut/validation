title: Keeping a lab reliable
source: v1
points:
  - Remote power control (PDU) and serial consoles so a hung device can be recovered without walking to the lab.
  - "Health checks before every run: device responds, cell is up, path loss as expected."
  - Monitoring and alerts for hosts, devices and services.
  - Equipment booking or locking so two tests do not fight over one instrument.
  - Inventory of what is connected where, kept up to date.
  - A clear owner per bench and a simple way to report a broken setup.
say: A reliable lab is remote-recoverable, monitored, and never shares equipment silently.
