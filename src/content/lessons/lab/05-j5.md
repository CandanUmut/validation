title: Keeping a lab reliable

points:
- <i>In practice:</i> remote power control (PDU) and serial consoles, so a hung device can be recovered without walking to the lab.
- '<i>In practice:</i> health checks before every run: device responds, cell is up, path loss as expected.'
- <i>In practice:</i> monitoring and alerts for hosts, devices and services.
- <i>In practice:</i> equipment booking or locking, so two tests never fight over one instrument.
- <i>In practice:</i> an inventory of what is connected where, kept current.
- <i>In practice:</i> a clear owner per bench and a simple way to report a broken setup.
say: A reliable lab is remote-recoverable, monitored, and never shares equipment silently.
trap: 'The most expensive lab failure is the silent one: a bench that looks healthy but measures wrong. Health checks must check levels, not just power-on.'
