title: dB math in your head
source: practice
points:
- 'dB is a ratio: <b>+3 dB ≈ double</b> (10<sup>0.3</sup> = 1.995), <b>+10 dB = ten times</b>, −3 dB ≈ half.'
- 'dBm is power relative to 1 mW: 0 dBm = 1 mW, 10 dBm = 10 mW, 20 dBm = 100 mW, 23 dBm ≈ 200 mW (power class 3), 30 dBm = 1 W.'
- dBi is antenna gain relative to an isotropic antenna.
- Thermal noise is <b>−174 dBm/Hz</b> (at room temperature). Noise floor = −174 + 10·log10(bandwidth in Hz) + noise figure.
- '<i>Calculation:</i> a 5 MHz LTE carrier occupies 4.5 MHz: −174 + 66.5 = −107.5 dBm; with a 7 dB noise figure, about −100.5 dBm.'
- '<i>Calculation:</i> RSRP is per resource element; full-carrier power is higher by 10·log10(12 × RBs): for 25 RBs, 24.8 dB.'
say: Three dB doubles, ten dB is ten times, and noise is −174 dBm per hertz before bandwidth and noise figure.
trap: Never add dBm to dBm. Add dB to dBm, or convert to milliwatts first.
