title: dB math in your head
source: v1
points:
  - dB is a ratio. <b>+3 dB</b> is double, <b>+10 dB</b> is ten times, -3 dB is half.
  - dBm is power relative to 1 mW. 0 dBm = 1 mW, 10 dBm = 10 mW, 20 dBm = 100 mW, 23 dBm = 200 mW, 30 dBm = 1 W.
  - dBi is antenna gain relative to an isotropic antenna.
  - Thermal noise is <b>-174 dBm/Hz</b>. Noise floor = -174 + 10 log10(bandwidth) + noise figure.
  - "Example: 4.5 MHz (a 5 MHz LTE carrier) gives -174 + 66.5 = -107.5 dBm. With a 7 dB noise figure the floor is about -100.5 dBm."
  - RSRP is per resource element. Full-carrier power is higher by 10 log10(12 x number of RBs). For 25 RBs that is about 24.8 dB.
say: Three dB doubles, ten dB is ten times, and noise is minus 174 dBm per hertz before bandwidth and noise figure.
trap: Do not add dBm to dBm. Add dB to dBm.
